01The Challenge

UpneX Solutions is hiring its first Social Media Intern — a freshers-only, remote, paid role. The challenge: make two shorts for TenantNex (tenantnex.com), our consumer product that prepares state-specific documents for US tenants. Our brands grow on one engine: short, honest, genuinely useful video.

Your clock is your own: the moment you register, the full brief lands in your inbox and your personal 48 hours begin. Two 30–40 second vertical shorts, submitted before your own deadline. We remind you at 24 hours and again in your final hour.

Use any AI tools and any style — talking-head, faceless captions, animation, screen-record, skit, explainer. Every entry must have a hook in the first 2 seconds, real substance, and one soft CTA. And keep it evergreen — no prices, no dates, nothing time-specific. Speed and judgment are part of the test, because that's the actual job.

The best entries get published on our official channels — some while the contest is still running. The best creator gets hired.

02The Two Briefs

Short A
The Security Deposit
A US tenant moved out weeks ago and the landlord hasn't returned their deposit. Teach them one genuinely useful thing about getting it back. Educational, plain English, no legal citations, no promises.
Short B
The Lease Exit
A US tenant needs to leave their lease early and is afraid of what it will cost. Give them one clear, honest piece of guidance. Same rules: useful first, truthful always.

The full brief pack — audience notes, approved fact sheet, and the do/don't claims rules — lands in your inbox the moment the sprint starts. Every claim in your video must be provable. That's not a formality; it's the house rule.

09Ground Rules

Truth first: every factual claim in your video must be provable and consistent with the fact sheet in the brief. No guarantees, no invented statistics, no legal citations, no advice framed as legal counsel.

Evergreen only: never mention any price, date, month, season or news event — entries must stay reusable years from now, exactly as they are.

Original work: everything must be your own. AI tools may assist — encouraged, even — but no stock-template rip-offs and no reused client work.

Publishing licence: by submitting, you grant UpneX Solutions OPC Pvt Ltd a perpetual, royalty-free licence to publish, adapt and use your submissions as the company's digital assets, effective immediately — including strong entries that do not win. Do not post contest entries publicly on your own accounts before results are announced.
